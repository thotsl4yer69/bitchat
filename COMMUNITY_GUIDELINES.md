# BitNow Community Guidelines

BitNow is an adults-only proximity dating and meeting product. These rules apply to profiles, signals, messages, media, and any other content shared through BitNow.

## 18+ only

You must be at least 18 years old to use BitNow. Do not create, request, share, sexualize, or discuss sexual content involving anyone under 18. Any indication that a user is under 18 should be treated as a safety issue and reported.

## Consent is required

A profile, nearby status, signal, match, conversation, previous interaction, relationship, or meeting does not create consent. Consent must be voluntary, specific, informed, ongoing, and can be withdrawn at any time.

## No sexual services or exploitation

BitNow must not be used to buy, sell, advertise, broker, or solicit prostitution, sexual services, human trafficking, exploitation, or coercive arrangements. Do not offer money, goods, housing, drugs, employment, transport, or other benefits in exchange for sexual activity.

## No pornographic or non-consensual content

Do not use profiles or public-facing BitNow surfaces to distribute pornographic material, graphic sexual content, sexualized nudity, non-consensual intimate material, voyeuristic content, or sexual content involving a person who did not agree to its creation or distribution.

## No harassment, threats, or stalking

Do not threaten, intimidate, repeatedly contact after rejection, impersonate, stalk, doxx, blackmail, extort, or pressure another person. Respect blocks and requests to stop communicating.

## No hate or targeted abuse

Do not attack or degrade people because of protected or personal characteristics. Dating preferences may be expressed respectfully; harassment and dehumanizing content are not acceptable.

## No deceptive or dangerous conduct

Do not impersonate another person, intentionally misrepresent age, use another person's images without permission, distribute malware, scam users, seek financial credentials, or use BitNow to facilitate violence or other serious harm.

## Location and privacy

Do not publish another person's home address, exact live location, workplace schedule, phone number, private identity information, or other sensitive information without their permission. BitNow deliberately avoids publishing an exact encounter map; users should not attempt to defeat that privacy model.

## Blocking and reporting

Users can block another peer locally. Blocking removes that peer from BitNow discovery and stops automatic profile responses on that device. A production App Store release must also provide a private reporting channel that can reach the BitNow moderation/support operator without exposing sensitive reports publicly.

Reports involving minors, credible threats, trafficking, exploitation, non-consensual intimate material, or immediate danger should receive the highest priority. Where required by law, the service operator may preserve or disclose report information to appropriate authorities.

## Enforcement

BitNow's peer-to-peer architecture means a local block cannot erase information another person has already received or guarantee a network-wide ban. Where a project-operated reporting/moderation service exists, action may include warnings, support intervention, removal from project-operated services, or other proportionate measures available to the operator.

These rules are part of the release contract for BitNow. Product copy, store metadata, and future features must remain consistent with them.
